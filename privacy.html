Privacy notice.

Last updated: 20 November 2026.

EDZ Barber is an independent barbering service based inside Novo Studios, 47–51 Norfolk Street, Cambridge CB1 2LD. Contact edzbarber.bookings@gmail.com with questions about your personal information.

What we collect

When you send an appointment request, we collect your name, email address, mobile number, chosen service, preferred date and time, and any optional notes. Please do not include sensitive personal details in notes.

Why we use it

We use this information to respond to your enquiry, check appointment possibilities, arrange barbering services, and communicate about your booking by email or WhatsApp Business. Sending a booking request does not subscribe you to marketing communications.

Legal basis

We primarily process appointment information to take steps you request before arranging a service. We may retain necessary business records for legal and accounting purposes.

Service providers

The booking form uses Web3Forms to send your request to an email inbox with Google Gmail. These services process information to deliver and store messages. Read Web3Forms' privacy policy and Google's privacy policy. Some processing may occur outside the UK subject to applicable safeguards.

Retention and rights

We keep your information only as long as necessary for enquiries, appointments, and applicable record-keeping duties. You may request access, correction or deletion of your information, subject to legal exceptions, by emailing us. You may also contact the UK's Information Commissioner's Office.

Optional advertising cookies

If you choose to accept, we load Google Ads and Meta Pixel to measure visits and successful booking requests. These services may process device and browsing data. We do not include the name, phone number, email address or form notes in advertising conversion events. Declining optional tracking does not affect booking. Your choice is stored in your browser. Use to change it. See Meta and Google privacy policies for more information.
