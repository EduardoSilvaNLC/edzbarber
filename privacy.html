Privacy notice.

Last updated: 6 November 2026.

EDZ Barber is an independent barbering service based inside Novo Studios, 47–51 Norfolk Street, Cambridge CB1 2LD. Contact edzbarber.bookings@gmail.com with any questions about your information.

What we collect

When you use our booking request form, we collect your name, email address, phone number, selected service, preferred date and time, and any additional notes you choose to share. Please do not include sensitive personal details in the notes.

What we use it for

We use your information to answer your enquiry, check possible appointments and arrange the barbering service you request. We may use your phone number to contact you about your booking. We do not automatically add you to a marketing mailing list.

Legal basis

Our main basis for processing appointment requests is taking steps at your request before arranging a service. We may also retain necessary business records to comply with applicable obligations.

Service providers

Our form uses Web3Forms to deliver requests to a Google Gmail inbox. These services process your information to transmit and store messages. You can read Web3Forms' privacy notice and Google's privacy policy. Some data processing may take place outside the UK, subject to relevant transfer safeguards.

Storage and retention

We keep booking correspondence only as long as reasonably needed to manage enquiries and appointments or meet legal and accounting obligations. We delete messages we no longer need.

Your rights

You can email us to request access to, correction of or deletion of your data, subject to any applicable exceptions. You can also contact the UK's Information Commissioner's Office.

Security and spam prevention

We use hCaptcha, provided through Web3Forms, to reduce automated and fraudulent booking requests. The security check may process device, browser and network data to verify requests, independently of optional advertising consent. See hCaptcha's privacy policy for details.

Advertising measurement and optional cookies

With your permission, we use Google Ads and Meta Pixel to measure visits and successful booking requests generated by our advertisements. Google and Meta may use cookies and process device, browser and advertising-click information for this purpose. We do not include your name, email address, telephone number or booking notes in our advertising conversion events. We load neither Google Ads nor Meta Pixel before you consent, and you can still submit a booking request if you decline. Your optional choice is stored in your browser. You can change it using . Withdrawing consent prevents these tags from running on subsequent page views. For more detail, see Meta’s privacy policy and Google’s privacy policy.
