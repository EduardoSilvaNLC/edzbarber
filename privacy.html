Privacy Notice

Last updated: 21 November 2026.

EDZ Barber is an independent barbering service based inside Novo Studios, 47–51 Norfolk Street, Cambridge CB1 2LD, United Kingdom. You can contact us about your data at edzbarber.bookings@gmail.com.

Information collected

When you request an appointment, we collect your name, phone number, email address, requested service, date and time, and any optional notes. Please do not include sensitive information in your notes.

How we use the information

We use your details to reply to your enquiry, check our appointment diary, and communicate about your requested service by email or WhatsApp Business. Booking enquiries do not automatically subscribe you to marketing.

Legal basis

We generally process appointment enquiries to take steps at your request before providing a service, and may retain records where required for legal or accounting obligations.

Service providers

We use Web3Forms to transmit requests to the barber's email inbox, and email services to receive and store them. Processing may occur outside the UK with applicable safeguards.

Retention and rights

We keep personal information for as long as necessary to manage enquiries, appointments and applicable record-keeping obligations. To request access, correction, deletion, or to ask a data protection question, please email us. You can also raise a complaint with the UK Information Commissioner's Office.

Optional advertising tools

If you accept optional tracking, Google Ads and Meta Pixel can record page visits and successfully sent booking requests. We do not deliberately send your name, phone number, email address or booking notes with those events. These tools will not load until you accept. Declining does not prevent booking.
